Designer& creativetechnologist

I am a designer and creative technologist who designs and builds digital and physical products.

I like things that feel handmade even when they run on code. My work lives somewhere between a printed zine and a prototype: loud type, soft color, and interactions that reward poking around.

Lately that means websites that behave like objects, small tools for artists, and physical products with a digital twin. I move between sketching, soldering, and shipping.

Before this: school, a lot of late nights in Illustrator, and a few freelance projects I am still proud of. Currently open to collaborations and full-time roles.
